# Stateful lifecycle qualification

Gate: **VOID_LIFECYCLE_QUALIFIED**

Candidate sites and cavities use separate typed registries. Birth accumulates a Poisson multi-hit completion clock and a first-passage hazard. A firing birth creates an embryo. Stabilization and healing then compete on existing embryos; healing leaves no hole, while stabilization creates exactly one stable cavity and consumes the site. Candidate weight appears in `birth_intensity` only.

Stable growth is controlled by the cavity object and its defect inventory. When vacancy delivery and plastic accommodation are both required, V1 uses `1/(1/Rdiff + 1/Rplast)`. The local defect provider is explicit, replaceable, disabled by default, and uncalibrated. Snapshot/rollback and the versioned dictionary contract include clocks, thresholds, RNG state, inventory, lineage, and transaction identity.
