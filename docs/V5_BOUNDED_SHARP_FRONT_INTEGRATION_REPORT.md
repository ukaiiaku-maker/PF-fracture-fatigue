## Adapter diff

- Generalized the established directional J/K measurement to an explicit
  `branch_id`; the old `_direct_measurement` wrapper retains its prior active-tip
  behavior.
- At child creation, uses the ordinary `FrontEngine.__init__ -> reset` policy and
  checkpoints the canonical engine fields. The orphan `r_tip_m` state and branch
  mirror are removed.
- Cavity-surface first passage remains on `cavity_boundary_tensor` and does not
  use child radius or child engine state.
- After child creation, continuation uses the same directional J-to-K provider,
  restores the child `FrontEngine`, evaluates both cleavage and emission paths,
  and executes the existing `FrontEngine.step` renewal after accepted directional
  first passage.
- `R_void` is not an argument to, or input of, the child constitutive response.
  It can affect child continuation only through a newly equilibrated structural
  field and its resulting J/K measurement.

No constitutive equation, crystallographic eligibility rule, scientific
tolerance, barrier law, or empirical stress multiplier was added or changed.
