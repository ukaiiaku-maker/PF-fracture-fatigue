## Open release boundaries

The branch does not yet provide qualified shared top-level executors for every
accepted bulk/cavity/hold/V3.6 composition or for fixed-range branching. The
native launchers are preserved and the resolver no longer drops their roles,
but these gaps prevent a claim that every accepted combination operates from
the shared `run` API. A same-state immutable base/current coupled mechanics
trajectory and full interruption/restart continuations for all topology
families also remain open. The corrected observer does not invent missing
within-cycle phase samples in older CSVs.
