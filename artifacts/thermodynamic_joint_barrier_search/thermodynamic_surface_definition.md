# Thermodynamic surface definition

At `T_r=300 K`, cleavage is the exact P25 or P40 EXP-floor surface plus one low-stress guard; emission is the exact A_NATIVE surface. The extension is `G=G_r-(T-T_r)S_r+DeltaCp[(T-T_r)-T ln(T/T_r)]`, `S=S_r+DeltaCp ln(T/T_r)`, `H=G+TS`, and `V=-dG/dsigma`. Entropy and volume are derived from this single surface. Attempt frequencies remain fixed and no entropy prefactor is added.
